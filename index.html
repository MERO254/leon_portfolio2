About

I am Leon Juma, known online as Legendary Killer, a Kenyan model, content creator and campus media personality working across fashion, entertainment and youth culture. I am based in Eldoret.

As Mr University of Eldoret, I stand for confidence, self-expression, leadership and the lively spirit of campus life. I bring that energy to the runway, to the camera and to my content.

My work joins modelling, leadership and digital storytelling. I am building a personal brand that speaks to young audiences and gives fashion brands, businesses, event organisers and fellow creatives a partner who understands them.

I am a versatile presence in Kenya’s campus entertainment and fashion scene, and I am ready for professional work beyond it.

“Confidence in every step. Identity in every frame. Legacy in every move.”

Title and leadership

Mr University of Eldoret (Mr UOE)

I hold the title of Mr University of Eldoret, a role that sits at the centre of my public identity and modelling journey. It gives me a platform to represent the university, take part in campus entertainment activities and encourage creativity, confidence and involvement among students.

Modelling chairperson

I also serve as a modelling chairperson. That means organising modelling activities, coordinating members and supporting emerging talent as they grow in the modelling community.

The role reflects my interest in teamwork, organisation, communication and the growth of Kenya’s fashion and entertainment industry.

Legendary Killer

Legendary Killer is my creator brand. It brings together TikTok entertainment, memes, edits, relatable youth content and campus-related entertainment. Through it I build an engaging digital presence, connect with audiences and explore creative partnerships with businesses and brands.

UOE Celebrities

I am associated with UOE Celebrities, a campus entertainment and media platform for the University of Eldoret community. It covers outfits, campus chronicles, trending news, entertainment and business promotions.

The platform links my interests in content creation, digital communication and campus culture, and it is a natural home for collaborations with businesses, event organisers, fashion brands and student-led initiatives.
